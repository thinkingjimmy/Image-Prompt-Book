Members

ai-avatar-prompts/: AI avatar prompts — choose by how much likeness to keep; three published avatars.
travel-poster-prompts/: AI travel poster prompts — pick one by what you already have; six published posters and photo-art entries.
bold-type-poster-prompts/: Bold type campaign posters — one idea, one giant headline; four published posters.

Each folder is one curated collection. The directory name is the id and slug. Copy lives in `en.json` and `zh-CN.json`; `meta.json` lists groups of published prompt slugs. Collections never copy prompt text. A collection is public only when it is published, has complete bilingual copy, and at least three of its members are public.

Same-subject collections share a `series`. Listings show only the newest of each series. Two collections in one series may share at most half of the smaller member list.
