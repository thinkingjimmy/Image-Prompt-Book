[Overall style]

Use a mid-20th-century modernist retro editorial illustration style, blending the design language of vintage book covers, vintage magazine covers, film noir and geometric graphic design.

The image should be:

black-dominant;
retro and premium;
rational and restrained;
simple and striking;
geometrically simplified;
strongly cover-like.

Do not default to a detective theme; choose the most fitting imagery for the topic, while always keeping the same retro modernist visual language.

[Color rules]

Keep the whole image strictly within 3 colors:

1. Black as the absolute main color, covering most of the background or subject;
2. White or off-white as the secondary color;
3. Only 1 bright accent color.

The accent color can be one of red, green, blue, orange, purple or yellow, chosen to suit the topic.

Do not use several bright colors at once;
no highly saturated multicolor;
no complex gradients;
use the accent color only on part of the subject, key words of the title, geometric shapes or a few emphasis elements.

Arrange the composition to fit the chosen aspect ratio.

Choose automatically to suit the topic:

top and bottom sections;
a poster layout led by a single subject.

Keep only:

one core subject;
one visual center;
one main color focus;
one set of main title information.

Balance the subject and the text; keep the structure clear and do not overcrowd it.

[Subject treatment]

Distill the single most representative core image from the topic.

Render the subject with:

geometric outlines;
large flat color blocks;
silhouette-style light and shadow;
simplified features and details;
a little exaggerated proportion;
retro editorial illustration.

People, products, buildings, robots, service scenes or abstract concepts should all be reduced to clear, simple, easily recognized visual symbols.

No realistic photography;
no 3D;
no anime style;
no complex mechanical detail;
no piles of unrelated decoration.

[Typography]

The main title must stand out as the first visual center of the image.

Set the Chinese main title in a typeface with strength, a book-cover feel or an editorial feel.

The English subtitle can use an elegant serif or a clean uppercase face.

Keep supporting text to a minimum, only what is necessary.

Do not use lots of tags, specs, icons, numbering or captions.

[Texture]

Add slight vintage paper grain, screen-print, old book-cover or worn-ink texture.

Keep the grain restrained; do not make it shabby, dirty or badly faded.

Keep the whole image crisp, flat and easy on the eye.

[Edges]

The image must be full bleed.

Content runs straight to all four edges of the canvas.

No white border;
no card-style frame;
no rounded white edges;
no margins around the image;
no light-colored outer rim.

[Do not]

No neon cyberpunk;
no modern tech blue-purple gradients;
no multicolor palettes;
no complex lighting effects;
no glass textures;
no metallic 3D;
no lots of small icons;
no information overload;
no generic internet promo-poster look.

The final image should look like a premium retro feature cover or a classic book cover:

black dominant;

only one bright color;
a simple, powerful subject;
