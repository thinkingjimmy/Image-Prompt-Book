Blow up {{subject}} into a whole landscape, and let tiny people climb along it, walk across it, stop to rest, or even lean right out of the picture frame. A flower can become a mountain, a cup can become a city; an ordinary object suddenly feels a little surreal.

Then pull the image together with {{background}}, a screen-print texture and clean, clear lines. Scatter the figures loosely rather than lining them up neatly, as if they really live and move around in this “giant-object world.”
